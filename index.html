Tsunehiko Tanaka

I am a full-time Software Engineer at IBM, where I work on agent memory for watsonx Orchestrate. I am also a part-time Adjunct Researcher at Waseda University, where I earned my Doctor of Engineering degree in March 2026.

My research focuses on sequential decision-making, drawing on machine learning, computer vision, and natural language processing. Recently, I have been exploring how embodied agents can use working memory to plan and act. In my work on long-horizon robotic manipulation, I combined vision-language models (VLMs) with task and motion planning (TAMP).

During my PhD, I trained a large language model (LLM) to turn written board game rules into executable programs. I used Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) with rewards based on grammar checks and simulated gameplay. I also developed the Return-Aligned Decision Transformer (RADT), an offline reinforcement learning (RL) method that uses a target score to control an agent's performance for applications such as tuning the difficulty of AI opponents.
